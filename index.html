— In partnership

A glass with Laurent-Perrier.

A long-standing house collaboration. Four of Maison Laurent-Perrier's emblematic cuvées are poured daily in the lounge, paired with seasonal canapés composed by our chef — Gruyère gougères, smoked trout rillettes and confit shallot tartlets.

A quiet moment of celebration before take-off — discreetly served at your table or at the champagne bar.

— In partnership

A Lindt Grand Cru moment.

Maîtres chocolatiers since 1845. The lounge serves single-origin Lindt Grand Cru selections — Ecuador 75 %, Madagascar 70 %, Trinidad 65 % — paired with our pastry chef's seasonal compotes and house-made pralinés.

Find them at the dessert station throughout the day, or request a small tasting flight at your table.

— In partnership

A taste of Le Gruyère AOP.

Crafted by alpine artisans since the 12th century. Three age statements daily — 6, 12 and 24 months — from family-run dairies in Fribourg and Vaud, alongside Swiss honey, walnut bread and seasonal compotes.

A quiet expression of the Swiss Pre-Alps, presented each morning at the cheese counter.
